Fair chemical pricing

Customers compare your price to the store shelf, not to your cost. Price against the shelf, then check that the markup covers your time.

Liquid chlorine, 1 gal

Suggested delivered: $7–9

Store shelf $6–8 (Walmart $5.97, Lowe's $7.98, Leslie's about $7.50 in a 4-pack). Pros pay $2–5, lowest in Florida.

Muriatic acid, 1 gal

Suggested delivered: $11–14

Store shelf about $10 (Walmart $9.97). Check the strength: some jugs are 14.5%, not 31.45%.

Markup isn't the same as margin

Markup is added on top of your cost. Margin is the share of the price you keep. Pool stores aim for about 40% margin overall, which is a 67% markup. Distributors run about 30% margin.

Rules that keep prices feeling fair

  • Premium members pay about the store shelf price, with free delivery. That's the easy sell: same price, no trip.
  • Basic customers pay the same shelf price plus the delivery fee. Don't add both a big markup and a fee.
  • Keep heavy items like chlorine and salt within about 10–15% of the local shelf. People know those prices.
  • Take more margin on items people buy rarely and don't price-check, like stabilizer and test strips.
  • Recheck shelf prices each spring. Chlorine prices moved a lot during the 2026 shortage.
