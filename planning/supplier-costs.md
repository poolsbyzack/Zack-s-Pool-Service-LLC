## Typical order

2 gal liquid chlorine + 1 gal muriatic acid = **$8.83 cost**. At shelf (~$24) that's **~$15 profit before delivery costs** (a stop costs ~$7 in time and gas).

## Questions these raise

- **Repackaging bulk bags and buckets.** Chlorine, shock and algaecide are EPA-registered pesticides; repackaging or relabeling them generally requires EPA registration, and every chemical needs proper hazard labels. Baking soda and salt are simpler, but still need labels. Options: sell only in original containers, find the same products in consumer sizes, or dose it yourself on site ("drop and dose"). Check with your distributor and state ag department before scooping anything into smaller bags.
- **Accessories** (dispensers, scoops, hoses): add an "extras" list to the app?
